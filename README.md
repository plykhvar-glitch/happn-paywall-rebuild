# happn — Our Plans, personalized from the onboarding

**Live page: https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/happn-paywall-rebuild/**

happn's Our Plans paywall (Premium and Supreme), rebuilt in its own design and fed by what the
onboarding already asks: the first name, the favourite neighbourhood, and the button that opened the
paywall. Change them under the phone and the paywall rewrites itself. Flip **Today** to see the
version that ships now. English, French and Spanish, dark (as shipped) and light, plus a 30-second
walkthrough.

## The five changes

| # | Change | Today | Now | Expected impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | A second chance on close | The X closes Our Plans | Closing Supreme offers Premium, 3 months for 39,99 €; closing Premium offers 1 month for 29,99 € | ARPU +10–15% |
| 2 | Their name and neighbourhood on the photo | "Send unlimited Likes and see who Likes you" for everyone | "Lucía, send unlimited Likes to everyone you cross in Chamberí" | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | The feature they tapped, first | One benefit order whatever opened the paywall | Opened from Rewind, Boost, Travel Mode…: that row leads the list and the headline | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Your proof next to the price | Editor's choice, +390K subscribers and L.'s story a full scroll below the plans | One row under the plans with 4.2 ★, and L.'s story right under it | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | The saving said, the Supreme gap stated | "13,33 €/months", no total | "39,99 € · Save 56%", and "Only 6,66 €/month more than Premium" | CR +3–8% · ARPU +5–10% |

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps —
not measured lift for happn.*

Prices, plans, benefits, the +390K figure, Editor's choice and L.'s quote come from happn's own
in-app screens; the 4.2 rating from its App Store listing (Spain and France); the logo from
happn.com. Savings are computed from happn's own prices.
