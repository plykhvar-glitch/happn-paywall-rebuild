What this is

We went through happn’s onboarding as a new member, then rebuilt the screen where Premium and Supreme are decided.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We installed happn, answered every onboarding question, from hobbies and Teasers to a favourite neighbourhood, reached Our Plans, read your App Store listing and happn.com, and rebuilt that screen.

It is already a strong paywall: two tiers, three durations with Popular in the middle, Editor’s choice, 390K subscribers and a real member’s story. What it does not do is talk to the person in front of it. Your onboarding just asked for a first name and a favourite neighbourhood, and the app knows what was tapped to open Our Plans. The paywall uses none of it, your proof sits a full scroll below the prices, and closing it ends the conversation.

We did not redesign your paywall

Your dark sheet, the gold Premium and coral Supreme tabs, the photo card, the Standard / Popular / Save cards, your benefit icons and portraits, the Editor’s choice laurels and the gradient button all stay. Only the words, the order and two small additions change.

Our Plans, personalized from your own onboarding

Same sheet, same tiers, same prices, same art direction. Five changes, each fed by something your onboarding already asks, your app already knows or you already publish. Flip Today above the phone to see the version that ships now.

A second chance on close

Today
The X closes Our Plans, with no other offer.
Now
Uses: your own Premium 3-month plan (39,99 €) and 1-month plan (29,99 €), at the prices on your paywall. No new price, no discount, no countdown.
Potential outcome · based on Adapty dataARPU +10–15%from people who would have closed

Their name and neighbourhood on the photo

Today
Send unlimited Likes and see who Likes you on Premium and Boost visibility. Get more dates. on Supreme, the same for everyone.

The feature they tapped, first

Today
The same benefit order whatever opened Our Plans. Unlimited Rewinds is fifth, half under the fold; Travel Mode is second on Supreme.
Now

Your proof next to the price

The saving said, the Supreme gap stated

Today
13,33 €/months on Popular, no total, no saving. Supreme’s 19,99 € is never compared with Premium.
Now
Uses: your own prices. 39,99 € against three months at 29,99 € is a 56% saving; Supreme’s 59,99 € against 3 × 39,99 € is 50%; 19,99 € − 13,33 € is 6,66 € a month.
